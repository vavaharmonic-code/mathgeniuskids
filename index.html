EduMatrix Master - Interactive Step-by-Step Tutorial

How to maximize learning outcomes for Grades 1 to 12

1 Choose Role & Set Student Profile

Select whether you are a Student 🎓, Teacher 👨‍🏫, or Parent 👨‍👩‍👧 at the top bar. Fill in Student Name, Grade (1st through 12th Standard), and Boy/Girl selection. Snap or upload a photo, then edit/crop/rotate it using the photo editor.

2 Select Operation & Multiplication Tables

Pick Addition (+), Subtraction (-), Multiplication (×), or Division (÷). For primary grades, toggle Table numbers #1 to #20 or enter custom numbers above 12. For Higher Secondary Grades (9th to 12th), select Algebra, Trigonometry, Logarithms, or Calculus.

3 Practice with AI Voice Companion & Digital Writing

Use hands-free voice mode by clicking the microphone icon to speak your answers aloud. Use the digital slate or paper pad for rough calculations.

4 Dispatch Quizzes via WhatsApp & Export Reports

Teachers can enter a student's WhatsApp number to send customized quizzes directly. Parents and Teachers can export Excel spreadsheet reports or download printable PDF reports anytime.
